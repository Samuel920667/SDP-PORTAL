Privacy Policy

Student Delegatory Parliament, Veritas University

Effective:

1. What we collect

When you submit a petition we collect your name, student ID, faculty, email address, level, and the petition content you provide. This information is stored locally on the server running this portal.

2. How we use it

Your data is used solely to generate the formal petition PDF and to route your petition through the Student Delegatory Parliament to the Student Affairs Unit, Veritas University. It is not used for marketing or profiling.

3. Cookies

We use one functional cookie to remember your theme preference (light or dark mode). No tracking or analytics cookies are used.

4. Data sharing

Your petition details are shared only with the Student Affairs Unit, Veritas University, as part of the formal parliamentary procedure. No data is sold or shared with external third parties.

5. Data retention

Petition records are retained for the duration of the academic year in which they are submitted and may be archived thereafter in accordance with university record-keeping policy.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your petition record by contacting the Parliament Secretariat at the address listed in the footer.

7. Contact
